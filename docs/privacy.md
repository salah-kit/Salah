# Salah for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Salah calculates prayer times and the Qibla direction on your device. It has no
account, advertising, analytics or tracking. Salah does not operate a service that
receives your location, prayer settings or recordings.

## Data on your device

Salah stores your selected location, including its coordinates, city, country and
time zone; recent locations; calculation and appearance preferences; notification
choices; and setup progress. The app and its widget extension share settings in a
local App Group so widgets and controls can work without the app being open.

If you import audio, Salah copies the selected recording into its own storage and
creates a short excerpt for notification sounds. Neither copy is uploaded by Salah.
Changing a sound does not delete earlier imported copies. Playback state is shared
locally so widgets, Live Activities and Stop controls can reflect current playback.

Prayer notifications are scheduled locally. Widgets, notifications, Live Activities
and the system's playback controls can display prayer information while the device
is locked. You control their visibility in iOS settings.

## Apple services and network requests

If you allow current location, Salah uses Apple's Location Services while you use
the app. It sends the resulting coordinates to Apple's geocoding service to resolve
the city, country and time zone. If you search for a city, the search text and any
coordinates needed to resolve its time zone go to Apple Maps. These requests need a
network connection. Once a location is selected, prayer calculations work offline.

Apple processes these requests under its [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/),
[Location Services and Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/location-services/)
and [Apple Maps and Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/apple-maps/)
disclosures. Salah does not receive Apple's service logs or use those requests for
advertising or profiling. It does not share your data with analytics or advertising
providers.

Opening source, support, license or policy links uses your browser. The destination's
privacy policy applies to that visit. Information you choose to post in the public
GitHub project, including support reports, is public.

## Your choices and deletion

- Use a city instead of current location in **Salah → Settings → Location**. To stop
  location access, open **iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services →
  Salah** and select **Never**. The previously selected location remains available
  for offline calculations.
- Change notification permission in **iOS Settings → Notifications → Salah**.
  Individual prayer alerts and sounds can also be changed in Salah.
- Manage widgets, Live Activities and Lock Screen notification visibility through
  iOS settings and the Home Screen or Lock Screen editors.
- Salah retains its local files while installed, including recent locations,
  imported audio and any recovery copy of unreadable preferences. To delete this
  data, use **Delete App** for Salah. Removing its Home Screen icon or offloading
  the app keeps its data.

Device backups may include Salah's data according to your Apple backup settings.
Deleting the app does not remove an existing backup. You can manage or delete
backups in iCloud settings; [Apple's backup instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922)
explain the available controls. Salah has no server copy to access or delete and
does not provide cloud sync.

## On Apple TV

Salah for Apple TV also calculates prayer times on the device, with no account,
advertising, analytics or tracking. It stores your selected location, including its
coordinates, city, country and time zone; the cities you chose recently; your calculation,
sound and appearance choices; and setup progress on the Apple TV. It keeps a copy of the
settings the Top Shelf needs in a local App Group shared with its Top Shelf extension. The
extension draws the day's times and your city's name on the Home Screen while Salah's icon
is in the top row, where anyone watching can see them, and keeps those images in the App
Group's caches.

If you choose **Use This Apple TV's Location**, Salah asks for location access while you
use the app and requests one location. As on iPhone, it sends the coordinates to Apple's
geocoding service to resolve the city, country and time zone, and a city search sends what
you type to Apple Maps. Salah for Apple TV doesn't schedule notifications or import
recordings; its sounds play only while it's open.

To use a city instead, open **Salah → Settings → Location**. To stop location access,
turn it off for Salah in the Apple TV's Location Services settings; the selected location
remains available for offline calculations. Deleting Salah from the Apple TV removes its
settings, the Top Shelf's copy of them and its images.

## Questions and changes

Privacy questions can be raised through the [Salah support project](https://github.com/salah-kit/Salah/issues).
Do not include private coordinates or recordings in a public report. Changes to
this policy will be published here with a new effective date.
